What we polish

Any bright aluminium or stainless on the truck. Most jobs cover some or all of these:

  • Bullbars and bumpers
  • Fuel and hydraulic tanks
  • Alloy wheels
  • Grilles and headlight surrounds
  • Exhaust stacks and visors
  • Toolboxes, steps and trims

Why fleets polish

  • Presentation. Your trucks are rolling advertising. A polished rig tells customers you look after your gear.
  • Protection. Oxidation dulls and pits bare aluminium over time. Polishing removes it before it sets in.
  • Value. Well-kept brightwork helps a truck hold its look at trade-in or sale.
  • Driver pride. Drivers notice when their truck is looked after.

Common questions

Do you polish on site?

Yes. We come to your yard or depot with our own power and equipment, so the truck doesn't have to go anywhere.

How long does a polish take?

It depends on how much aluminium is on the truck and what condition it's in. We'll give you a time frame with your quote.

How much does aluminium polishing cost?

It's priced per truck based on the parts being polished and their condition. Send us photos or call for a straight quote.

Can you keep it looking polished?

Yes. Pair polishing with a regular fleet wash and we'll keep the brightwork clean between polishes.
