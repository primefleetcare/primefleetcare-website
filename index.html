What we do

Mobile. Flexible. Environmentally responsible.

We come to your depot with the gear and work around your operation.

Bunded wash zone & wastewater capture

When your site needs runoff contained, we bund the wash zone, capture all washwater — grease, detergent, road grime — and dispose of it responsibly off site. No stormwater runoff, no compliance risk. Itemised on your invoice when needed.

Eco-friendly products

Biodegradable cleaning chemicals throughout. Tough on grease and diesel soot — safe for the environment and compliant with WA environmental standards.

Fully mobile

We bring our own power and all the equipment, and use your yard's water supply. No tap on site? We can bring water in, itemised on your invoice. No wash bay needed.

Around your schedule

Early starts, late finishes, weekends. We work around your fleet's operating hours — your trucks keep running while we get them clean.

Services

Everything your fleet needs — done on-site.

Pick a core service, then add extras to suit your fleet. We bring the gear; all we need is a water tap on site.

Fleet wash

Full exterior wash of the cab, body, wheels and trailer. Book a one-off or a weekly, fortnightly or monthly program.

Full detailing

Inside and out. Exterior paint correction and protection, plus a deep clean of the cab: seats, dash, door trims and floor. Also available exterior-only or interior-only.

Aluminium polishing

Fuel tanks, wheels, bullbars, toolboxes and trims polished back to a bright finish. Removes oxidation and road film so your rigs stand out.

Add-ons

Add any of these to a wash or detail.

  • Engine & radiator degreaseCuts built-up grease that causes overheating and corrosion.
  • Underbody cleanMud, salt and road grime off the undercarriage before it rusts.
  • Interior wipe & vacuumA quick cab clean at the yard, so drivers aren't left waiting.
  • Trailer inside cleanDust, residue and odours out, ready for the next load.
  • Ceramic sealantLong-term paint protection that keeps trucks cleaner for longer.
  • Wastewater capture & disposalWash zone bunded and all washwater taken off site, for depots that need runoff contained.
